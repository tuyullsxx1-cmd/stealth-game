# THE VELVET CURTAIN

A top-down stealth game set in the Grand Velvet Theatre after midnight. You are
**Wren**, a cat-burglar. Slip past the ushers, steal the **Moon Masque** from the
prop vault, and reach the stage-door exit unseen.

Three levels — **The Lobby**, **The Auditorium**, **The Backstage Vault** — each with
patrolling ushers, vision cones, velvet curtains to hide in, footlight pools that
give you away, sweeping stage spotlights, and a laser grid guarding the vault.

## What is in the game

- **Real stealth systems** — patrol waypoints you can learn, vision cones with
  line of sight, a suspicion meter with patrol / suspicious / alarmed states,
  and recovery when you break line of sight and hide in shadow.
- **Light and shadow** — footlight pools and lantern glow make you seen fast;
  the wings and curtain shadows keep you hidden. The HUD shows your visibility
  live.
- **Noise** — sneak silently, walk quietly, sprint loudly with a visible noise
  ring. Footsteps get louder as a guard nears, so you can hear where they are.
- **Gadgets** — throw coins to lure a guard away, toss smoke pellets to blind
  a cone.
- **Loot and ghost runs** — ticket stubs to collect, one objective item per
  level, a locked exit until the objective is yours, and a +500 GHOST bonus
  for finishing unseen.
- **Sound** — fully procedural WebAudio: footsteps, coin clinks, pickup chimes,
  alarm stings, and a low theatre drone. Everything is offline; the game loads
  nothing over the network.

## How to play

Open `game/index.html` in a browser (or serve the `game/` folder). Click **START**.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD / Arrows | Move |
| Shift | Sneak (silent, slow) |
| Space | Sprint (loud, fast) |
| E | Hide in curtains / pick up / interact |
| Click | Throw a coin (lure a guard) |
| Q | Smoke pellet |
| R | Retry level |
| M | Mute |
| Esc | Pause |
